# Sermon management

Language used when preparing recordings and their associated sermon content.

## Language

**Teaching calendar**:
The church's schedule of planned sermon dates, titles, series, Bible readings, and preachers for each campus.

**Sermon draft**:
The editable sermon details and selected recording section being prepared for publication.

**Recording campus**:
The campus where the selected recording was made, which determines the corresponding planned preacher in the teaching calendar.

**Sermon title**:
The name of the individual sermon. The teaching calendar calls this the Sunday Topic.

**Topic tag**:
A reusable subject label describing a sermon, distinct from its title or series.

**Sermon transcript**:
A timestamped written record of the sermon audio, attached to the sermon itself.

**Sermon article**:
A separate written presentation of a sermon's message, rewritten for reading while preserving the preacher's meaning and theology.

# Giving

Language used when supporting apprentices and student ministers through Ev Church.

## Language

**Support profile**:
The church-managed name, photo, blurb, and email address of an apprentice or student minister. The same profile is presented on About and in the launcher.

**Designated fund**:
The Ev Church giving fund linked to a particular apprentice's or student minister's support profile.

**Directed giving**:
Giving to the designated fund of a support profile, with the fund shown before the amount question and fixed throughout that giving flow.

**Recurring giving setup**:
A donor's authorised arrangement for repeated gifts, including the amount, frequency, and starting date. Completing setup does not establish that a payment has succeeded.
_Avoid_: Completed gift when referring only to setup

**Support notification**:
An email to the apprentice or student minister for a designated fund when a donor completes recurring giving setup or a successful single gift through BlinkPay. It includes the donor's name and email, fund, gift amount, transaction fee and total, frequency, applicable start date, and completion time.

**Support contact email**:
The email address on a support profile, used for the person's published contact details and support notifications for their designated fund.
