# Sermon Content Pipeline

## Problem Frame

ev.church live-streams services on YouTube across two campus channels (Central and North) but currently only syncs audio from resources.ev.church. There is no video playback on the website, no way to watch just the sermon portion, and no text-based content generated from sermons. This limits discoverability (SEO), accessibility, and engagement for people who missed a service or want to go deeper.

## Requirements

### Phase 1: YouTube Video Ingestion & Matching

- R1. Pull recent videos from two YouTube channels (Central, North) on a scheduled basis
- R2. Match each YouTube video to the corresponding sermon record already synced from resources.ev.church (by date, title, or speaker)
- R3. Store YouTube video references (URL, campus, video ID) on the Sermons collection in Payload
- R4. On the sermon detail page, give users the option to watch video (choosing Central or North campus) or listen to audio
- R5. Video and audio are presented as equal choices; neither is forced as the default experience

### Phase 2: Transcription & Smart Player

- R6. Ingest transcriptions from Sunflower AI and associate them with the matching sermon/video (access method TBD -- see Outstanding Questions)
- R7. Auto-detect sermon start and end times from the transcript using structural cues: sermon begins after the Bible passage reading handoff; sermon ends after the speaker's closing prayer (the second prayer, not the opening one) before they introduce the next service item
- R8. Allow team members to manually adjust sermon start/end timestamps via the Payload admin panel
- R9. Build a custom video player (Video.js) that plays only the sermon segment, with the progress bar representing 0 to sermon length rather than the full livestream duration
- R10. Store the full transcript text associated with the sermon for use in Phase 3

### Phase 3: AI Content Generation

- R11. Use the Anthropic API to generate structured blog content from the sermon transcript
- R12. Generated blog posts must contain three sections:
  - **Engaging summary** of the sermon's message
  - **Reflection questions** drawn directly from questions raised in the sermon (not invented)
  - **Deeper scripture references** that came up in the sermon, with full CSB (Christian Standard Bible) text quoted
- R13. Content generation must be tightly constrained to the sermon transcript; the AI must not introduce theology, arguments, or claims not present in the source material
- R14. Scripture quotes must use the CSB translation, sourced from a local CSV Bible dataset
- R15. Generated blog posts auto-publish to the existing BlogPosts collection, with the ability for team to edit or unpublish after
- R16. Each AI-generated blog post must display a prominent notice making clear the content was generated by AI from the sermon recording
- R17. Blog posts and sermon pages are cross-linked for SEO: sermon page links to "Read the blog post", blog post links to "Watch the sermon"
- R18. Pipeline execution runs on a cron schedule, managed within the Payload admin panel
- R19. The Payload admin includes a pipeline management view: run status, manual re-triggers, token usage tracking for the Anthropic API
- R20. Anthropic API token usage should be managed efficiently, with awareness of budget/allocation

## Success Criteria

- Users can watch the sermon portion of a YouTube livestream on the website, choosing their campus
- AI-generated blog posts are published automatically after each sermon with no manual intervention required (but editable)
- Generated content faithfully represents the sermon without introducing external material
- AI-generated content is transparently disclosed to readers
- Sermon pages and blog posts cross-link, creating two indexed pages per sermon for improved SEO
- Pipeline runs reliably on schedule with visibility into status and costs from within Payload admin

## Scope Boundaries

- **In scope:** YouTube ingestion, transcript ingestion, custom player, AI blog generation, Payload admin pipeline management
- **Out of scope:** Replacing resources.ev.church as the primary sermon metadata source; it remains the source of truth for sermon records, speakers, topics, series, and scriptures
- **Out of scope:** Live captioning or real-time transcription on the website (Sunflower AI handles this independently)
- **Out of scope:** User accounts or personalisation (e.g., sermon history, bookmarks)
- **Out of scope:** Automatic social media posting or distribution of generated content
- **Deferred:** Sermon search powered by transcript content (valuable future extension but not part of this pipeline)

## Key Decisions

- **Phased delivery:** Three phases, each delivering standalone value. Phase 1 (YouTube video) can ship before transcription or AI content is ready
- **YouTube supplements, not replaces:** resources.ev.church remains the sermon record source; YouTube adds video to existing records
- **Multi-campus video choice:** Users pick Central or North campus video, or stick with audio
- **Auto-detect + manual override for timestamps:** AI suggests sermon boundaries from transcript structure; team can adjust in Payload
- **Auto-publish with edit-after:** Generated content publishes immediately; team can edit or unpublish. No approval gate
- **Cross-linked pages for SEO:** Separate blog post page per sermon rather than embedded content, maximising search surface
- **CSB translation:** Scripture quotes use the Christian Standard Bible from a local CSV dataset
- **AI transparency:** Prominent disclosure notice on all AI-generated blog posts
- **Grounded generation:** AI content must not deviate from what was said in the sermon; reflection questions must come from the sermon itself

## Dependencies / Assumptions

- YouTube Data API access for both campus channels (API key or OAuth required)
- Sunflower AI transcripts accessed by scraping their dashboard (login required, session history page, text download)
- CSB CSV Bible dataset availability and licensing confirmed for reproduction of passages
- Anthropic API key and token budget allocated for ongoing generation
- Payload job queue (already in use for sermon sync) can support additional scheduled tasks

- [Affects R14][Resolved] CSB allows quoting up to 1,000 verses without written permission, provided they don't exceed 50% of the work and don't quote a complete book. Attribution required: "Scripture quotations marked CSB have been taken from the Christian Standard Bible, Copyright 2017 by Holman Bible Publishers." Per-sermon blog posts will stay well within these limits. Still need to identify and obtain the CSV Bible dataset.
