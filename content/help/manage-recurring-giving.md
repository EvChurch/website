## View your giving

[Open your giving](/members/giving) and sign in. You can view your recurring arrangements and confirmed gift history.

## Update a card

For a card arrangement, open **Recurring gift options** and choose **Update card**. Complete the secure Stripe page, then return to your giving. This keeps the gift amount, fund and frequency unchanged. Updating a card does not restart a stopped arrangement.

If a payment email asks for authentication, use **Review your payment** in that email to complete your bank’s checks. Updating a card alone may not complete authentication for an existing payment.

## Stop or replace a recurring gift

Open **Recurring gift options**, then choose **Stop recurring gift** and follow the confirmation. Stopping prevents future scheduled charges; a payment already in progress may still complete.

To change the amount, fund, frequency or payment provider, first confirm that the old arrangement has stopped, then set up new giving. Do not start its replacement while cancellation is still being checked.

A stopped arrangement is retained in Ev’s records. It is not restarted automatically, including after a card dispute is resolved.
