## Open your approvals

[Open reimbursements](/members/reimbursements) and sign in. On your first visit, complete your personal payment settings to enter the reimbursement section, even if you are only reviewing claims.

Open [**Approvals**](/members/reimbursements/approvals). **Awaiting your approval** appears first and contains reports you can review now. **Active reports** shows the other reports you can see for your purposes. Paid, cancelled, and archived reports are under **Show older**.

## Review a report

1. On mobile, open the report using its number or choose **View report** from its three-dot menu. On a computer, use its number or the eye icon.
2. Check the campus, purpose, expense dates, categories, expense reasons, and amounts. Turn on the **Advanced** slider above the expenses to view Net, Gross, and each line's GST treatment.
3. Open each receipt using **Show receipt** on mobile or its eye icon on a computer and check it supports the amount claimed. Someone may claim part of a receipt or split it across categories.
4. Check **Activity** for the submission and any previous decisions.

Turn **Advanced** off to return to the single **Amount** column. This changes only your view; it does not edit the claim. On mobile, the slider is below **Expenses** on the left; on a computer, it is on the right beside the heading. **Activity** is hidden when there is no history.

Claimants see their own reports. Purpose approvers can see submitted reports for their assigned purposes. Bank details are available to finance, not purpose approvers.

**Pastor Expense Account** is a shared purpose available to anyone submitting reimbursements. It goes directly to finance and does not require purpose approval.

## Approve

Choose **Approve** when the report is ready. It then goes to **finance approval**. Your approval does not mark it as paid.

## Reject

Follow the prompt **Select any expenses that need changes, add one overall reason, then choose Reject.** Use the checkbox on the left of any expense that needs changing. Enter one clear overall reason in **Reason or note**, then choose **Reject**. You can also reject without selecting expenses when the issue affects the whole report. Clear all selected checkboxes before choosing **Approve**.

Rejection returns the entire report. The selected expenses are highlighted for the claimant and recorded in Activity alongside your reason. The claimant can edit any part of the report. No expenses can be paid until the whole report is approved.

![A sample finance review with the second expense selected and one overall rejection reason.](/images/help/reject-expense-lines.png)

The claimant can edit and resubmit. A resubmitted report starts the approval process again, including purpose approval.

## Assignment and reminders

Pending approval follows the person currently assigned to the purpose. If that assignment changes, the new approver handles pending reports. An approval already given remains complete.

If you are the claimant and would normally approve the report yourself, it goes to the configured senior pastor instead. Claims from the senior pastor use the configured alternate approver.

Email reminders are sent every two days while approval is pending. Open the report and approve or reject it to complete your review.

## If the claimant makes changes

A claimant can return a report to draft before finance approval is complete. Current approvals are disregarded, and you will need to review it again after they resubmit.

An archived report stops moving through the process. It cannot be approved while archived.
