## Choose your teams and dates

Open [My Service](/members/my-service) and choose **Schedule unavailability** beside **Upcoming unavailability**.

1. Under **Date range**, choose your first and last unavailable day. Both days are included. Choose today or future dates.
2. Add **Unavailability notes** if helpful, up to 100 characters. For example, enter “Away with family”.
3. Under **Serving teams**, check one or more teams. Nothing is selected initially. Choose **Select all** to check every team currently listed, then uncheck any that do not apply.
4. Choose **Save unavailability**. The same dates and notes apply to each selected team.

For example, select Welcome Team and Music Team for 20–22 December if you cannot serve on either team during those days. Other teams remain unaffected. **Select all** does not include teams you join later.

The team checkboxes and **Select all** button work with touch, keyboard and screen readers. With a keyboard, use Tab to move between controls and Space to toggle a focused checkbox.

## Check a save or retry

**Your unavailability has been saved.** means every selected team's save was confirmed. The form closes and **Upcoming unavailability** refreshes with a separate entry for each team.

If only some saves are confirmed, the form stays open and names those teams. Upcoming unavailability refreshes to show confirmed saves. The remaining teams may not have saved, or their save could not be confirmed. A connection timeout can also leave the outcome uncertain.

Keep the same teams, dates and notes and choose **Save unavailability** again. Matching saved entries are reused; missing entries are created. The selection, dates and notes stay in the form so you can retry. If your session has expired, sign in again before saving.

## Remove an entry

Under **Upcoming unavailability**, choose the remove button beside the team's date range, check the team and dates in **Remove unavailability?**, then choose **Remove**. Removing one entry leaves other teams' entries in place.

If scheduling is temporarily unavailable, try again later. If no serving groups are available, contact your serving team leader. Unavailability is read-only while impersonating a member.
