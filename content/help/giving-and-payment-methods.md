## Choose how to give

[Open giving](/give). Choose an amount, frequency and fund, then check your details. Directed giving opens with its fund already selected.

On the separate **How would you like to pay?** step, choose **Pay by bank** or **Debit or credit card**. Each option shows its payment fee and total before you open its provider page and authorise it. The gift amount goes to your chosen fund; the fee is shown separately. Unavailable methods are disabled.

**Pay by bank** uses BlinkPay and supports ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Kiwibank and Westpac. Continue to BlinkPay to authorise with your bank.

**Debit or credit card** opens secure card entry within the giving flow. Stripe handles your card details. Apple Pay or Google Pay may also appear on supported devices. Your bank may ask you to authenticate.

**Try manual bank transfer** is a smaller fallback link beneath the main choices in this giving flow, for when the other methods are not working. Follow the account and reference instructions. **I’ve set this up** records your acknowledgement; it does not confirm that Ev has received a payment.

## Recurring gifts

Choose every week, every two weeks or every month, and select a starting date. Check the gift, fee and total for each payment before authorising. Existing arrangements keep their agreed fees when settings change.

When you choose **Today** and pay by card, your first gift is paid immediately. Today remains available at any time; choosing your payment method later does not add a cutoff. Subsequent gifts follow your chosen schedule.

You receive a setup confirmation. A future-start arrangement is confirmed as scheduled; this does not mean its first payment has already happened.

For failed recurring card payments, recoverable failures are retried on days 1, 4 and 9. We email you when a card update or authentication is required. If recovery fails, the arrangement stops and we let you know. Missed gifts are not accumulated for collection later.

## Check an uncertain result

If the flow says it is still checking, do not start another payment or switch methods. Ev verifies the outcome with the provider. A payment confirmation is separate from the provider paying funds into Ev’s bank account, and is not an annual charitable tax receipt.
