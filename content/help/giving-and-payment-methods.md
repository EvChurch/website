## Choose how to give

[Open giving](/give). Choose an amount, frequency and fund, then check your details. Directed giving opens with its fund already selected.

In the [generosity calculator](/generosity/calculator), select a giving level under **Going forward**, then choose **Set up a gift** (or **Set up an additional gift** if you already give to General Fund). The launcher opens with just the extra General Fund amount needed to reach that level and its frequency filled in. It keeps your existing General Fund frequency, or uses monthly if there is no existing gift. Choose a starting date and complete your details before authorising payment. Your existing gifts continue; the additional gift covers only the increase.

In **Your household income**, enter each earner’s amount and frequency. Tick **Amount is after tax** if you are entering take-home pay; otherwise the calculator estimates take-home pay using its NZ tax settings and selected deductions. Giving percentages, the assessment and the **Going forward** table use household take-home income, including other income and Working for Families. The assessment and recommendations are shown in full without a **Read more** button. Below them, **Where to Start** includes **Set up your giving** with a **Start giving** button, **Faithful with Money**, and **Go deeper with budgeting**. The separate **Money tight?** section suggests speaking with your Connect Group leader and links to **Contact us** for support.

On the separate **How would you like to pay?** step, choose **Pay by bank** or **Debit or credit card**. Each option shows its payment fee and total before you open its provider page and authorise it. The gift amount goes to your chosen fund; the fee is shown separately. Unavailable methods are disabled.

**Pay by bank** uses BlinkPay and supports ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Kiwibank and Westpac. Continue to BlinkPay to authorise with your bank.

**Debit or credit card** opens secure card entry within the giving flow. Stripe handles your card details. Choose **Cancel** below the payment button to return to your saved gift choices. Cancel is unavailable while payment confirmation is in progress. Apple Pay or Google Pay may also appear on supported devices. Your bank may ask you to authenticate.

**Try manual bank transfer** is a smaller fallback link beneath the main choices in this giving flow, for when the other methods are not working. Follow the account and reference instructions. **I’ve set this up** records your acknowledgement; it does not confirm that Ev has received a payment.

## Give Sunday one-off gifts

**Choose Your Gifts** becomes available to everyone at 12:00 am on Saturday, 5 December 2026 (Auckland time). Before then, this button is available only to signed-in members of the Pastoral Team, Staff Team, Ministry Apprentices or Executive Member groups; otherwise it is disabled with **Return on Give Sunday to Give** underneath. Once the giving flow is open, checkout uses the normal payment checks without another Staff/Exec check.

On the [one-off giving page](/one-off-giving), choose **Choose Your Gifts** in the page header, beneath the introduction or below the opportunities to open the launcher. The first screen explains the four opportunities and the option to explore child sponsorship through Tearfund after completing your gift. Choose **Okay, let’s go** to begin, or **Give to greatest need** beneath the **or** divider to give one amount instead. Each opportunity has a numbered title and a progress pill, from **1/4** to **4/4**. Choose **$200**, **$1,000**, **$5,000**, or enter your own whole-dollar amount. Continue through Plant, Train, Grow and Build, or choose **Skip this opportunity**. Use the launcher’s Back arrow to return to the previous step.

You can choose **Give to greatest need** on the introduction screen, below an opportunity or on the review screen. This is a general gift that Ev can use wherever it is needed most. Its amount starts with the total of your selected gifts. You can change that amount, or use Back to keep your earlier choices. **Continue to payment** replaces those allocations with one Greatest Need gift and proceeds directly to payment. This route has no skip or review step.

The campaign totals show confirmed website gift allocations and refresh within a minute, on returning to the page, and after a gift is confirmed. They exclude pending gifts, test payments and transaction fees. These are cumulative gift amounts before refunds; refund adjustments and gifts recorded outside the website are handled separately in finance reporting.

After Build, **Review your gifts** shows all four opportunities, including skipped opportunities as **$0**, and your total. Use a pencil icon to edit an amount or remove that gift. Continue to payment when the details are correct. The payment step uses the normal giving bubbles: **I would like to give** with your total amount, **Just this once**, and **for Give Sunday**. Signed-in people with complete saved details go straight to payment methods; guests enter their name and email. The existing giving flow shows the available bank and card payment methods, and confirms any fee before payment. Your selected gifts make one payment to the Give Sunday fund, with each allocation retained for reconciliation. Campaign gifts do not currently offer the manual-transfer fallback. After verified payment, the confirmation shows your allocation breakdown, including **$0** for skipped opportunities. **Explore child sponsorship** opens Tearfund’s separate sponsorship page.

Choose **Start over** on the completion screen to begin again on the same device with no amounts selected. The completed gift remains recorded; starting over does not cancel or repeat it.

## Recurring gifts

Choose every week, every two weeks or every month, and select a starting date. Check the gift, fee and total for each payment before authorising. Existing arrangements keep their agreed fees when settings change.

When you choose **Today** and pay by card, your first gift is paid immediately. Today remains available at any time; choosing your payment method later does not add a cutoff. Subsequent gifts follow your chosen schedule.

You receive a setup confirmation. A future-start arrangement is confirmed as scheduled; this does not mean its first payment has already happened.

For failed recurring card payments, recoverable failures are retried on days 1, 4 and 9. We email you when a card update or authentication is required. If recovery fails, the arrangement stops and we let you know. Missed gifts are not accumulated for collection later.

## Check an uncertain result

Before payment, **Checking your browser…** means the security check is still running. Complete it if prompted. Continue becomes available after it succeeds. If the check cannot complete, choose **Retry security check**. If it keeps failing, allow Cloudflare’s security check in your browser or try another browser; no payment has been submitted at this stage.

If the page says your giving session could not be authorised, choose **Reload giving form** and check that you are signed into your church account. This message means payment was not started; do not keep clicking Continue on the same form.

If you leave card entry before a payment attempt begins, reopening giving restores the same unpaid card checkout. This does not create another gift or charge you.

If you cancel at BlinkPay, you return to the giving flow. Ev checks the saved payment with BlinkPay; once cancellation is confirmed, the flow shows **No gift was made.**

If the flow says it is still checking, do not start another payment or switch methods. Ev verifies the outcome with the provider. A payment confirmation is separate from the provider paying funds into Ev’s bank account, and is not an annual charitable tax receipt.
