## Start a course

Open [Courses](/members/courses) and sign in or create an account before browsing. You do not need to be a church member. Choose an available course and select **Start course** or **Start group**. Links from the public giving calculator and email invitations also take you through sign-in first.

Course pages use the same member navigation as the rest of the portal, with **Courses** beside **Giving**. On smaller screens, open **Member menu** to see the portal links.

## Continue learning

Courses you have started appear under **In progress**. Select **Continue** to return to your next lesson. Lessons unlock in order. You can return to completed lessons; later lessons unlock after you finish the current one.

Each lesson guides you through one reading or activity at a time. Use **Next** and **Back**, and follow **Step x of y** to see your place. Your place saves to your account, so you can continue on another device.

Choose **Finish lesson** on the final step. Completing every required lesson moves the course to **Completed**.

## Return or start again

Select **View course** under **Completed** to revisit earlier lessons. To start a fresh attempt, open the course under **Available** and select **Start course** or **Start group**. Your earlier attempt is retained.

If a course closes to new enrolments, you can still continue an existing attempt. Structural course changes apply to new attempts; your existing lessons and progress stay together.

## Your answers

Answers save automatically to your account. Wait for **Saved** before leaving, then sign in on any device to continue. There is no Submit button.

**Personal · Only you** means your answer stays private. All answers are private in an individual course. Use **A way in** for optional guidance.

If saving fails, your work stays on the screen; choose **Retry**. If another tab saved a newer answer, choose **Use saved answer** or **Save my version**. Your typing is kept until you choose.

Use **Delete answer** to clear an answer from your account. Required questions must be answered before marking a lesson complete.

## Build your budget

Enter your weekly take-home pay and planned amounts in NZ dollars. Use up to two decimal places. Category totals and the amount left update as you type; an over-budget amount does not prevent you from saving.

Use **Add a row** for another expense and **×** to remove a row. Your budget uses the same autosave and privacy rules as other answers. **Delete budget** clears the saved figures and restores the starting rows.

[Faithful with Money](/members/courses/faithful-with-money) has three lessons covering money, giving and building a budget. Start it from **Grow in generosity** or **Courses**, then sign in or create an account. Its money story, giving figures and budget are Personal. Use the separate [giving calculator](/generosity/calculator) to explore an amount; calculator figures do not transfer into your course.

## Learn with a group

Two people use the same group flow as a larger group. The person who starts it is the facilitator. Open **Your group** to invite someone by email. Invitations expire after seven days and must be accepted using the invited email address.

**Shared · Everyone in this group** is one answer the group can update. **Shared · Your own answer** is your answer visible to the group; open **Other answers** to read theirs. New members can see earlier shared answers. Personal answers remain private, including from the facilitator.

Facilitators can see lesson progress, remove participants, transfer facilitation, or close the group through **Manage group**. Closing stops invitations and editing but keeps answers readable. Leaving or removal ends shared-answer access; your personal answers stay in your account. The facilitator must transfer responsibility or close the group before leaving.

**Delete shared workbook** clears the group's collaborative answers after explicit confirmation. It does not delete participants' personal answers. Starting with different people creates a fresh course attempt.
