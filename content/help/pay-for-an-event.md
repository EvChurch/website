## Register and check your total

Find your event on [Events](/events) and choose **Register now**. Complete the registration questions and review the **Payment Summary**, including **Amount To Pay Today**. For example, one $30 ticket should show $30 to pay.

## Pay with BlinkPay

For events using bank payments, choose **Pay**, then **Continue to bank**. Keep the original registration window open. BlinkPay opens in a separate tab and shows **Pay Ev Church**, the event's payment details and the amount.

Select your bank and follow its instructions to authorise the payment. Return to the original registration window and wait for the registration confirmation. Opening BlinkPay alone does not complete your registration.

## Cancel or check an uncertain result

Use **Cancel payment** on BlinkPay if you do not want to proceed. The registration window shows **Payment not completed** after cancellation.

If you already authorised a payment and the registration has not confirmed, keep the original window open while it checks. Do not start another payment if your bank shows a debit or the outcome is uncertain. [Contact us](/contact) or the organiser listed on the event page so the payment can be checked first.

For an existing registration with a balance to pay, use its payment link from your confirmation or reminder email. Do not create a second registration to pay that balance.
